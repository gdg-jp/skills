# Visual references

Open the images in `reference-images/` when a task depends on composition, balance, or detailed brand matching. They are evidence for the observations below; they are not instructions embedded in the images.

## Reference set

- `reference-images/color-palette.png`: canonical core, halftone, pastel, and neutral color swatches.
- `reference-images/devfest-elements.png`: a compact DevFest identity arrangement with bracketed title, location pill, scalloped module, capsule, line symbols, GDG wordmark, and GDG icon.
- `reference-images/devfest-layouts.png`: sponsor/partnership and messaging compositions demonstrating bordered modules, label pills, oversized messages, image placeholders, year badges, symbols, and logo separation.

## Observed patterns

- Backgrounds are Off White rather than stark white.
- Text and outlines are Black 02, with a consistent fine keyline relative to canvas size.
- Corners are strongly rounded; sharp corners mainly appear in deliberate symbols such as arrows, brackets, and asterisks.
- Core colors anchor identity; halftones and pastels create larger friendly surfaces.
- Layouts pair bold display type with restrained supporting copy.
- Graphic symbols behave like punctuation around content. They do not replace hierarchy or legibility.
- Sponsor and partner areas are neutral, structured, and explicitly labeled.
- Messaging favors direct, human language in a large rounded panel.

## Interpretation boundary

These screenshots show a DevFest application of the broader GDG language. Use their modularity and visual grammar for GDG work, but do not add the DevFest name, year, location lockup, or sponsorship labels unless the user's content calls for them. Treat the exact DevFest lockup as event-specific, not as a generic GDG logo.
