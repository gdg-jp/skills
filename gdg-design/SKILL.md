# GDG Design

Create work that is recognizably GDG: friendly, modular, bold, and technically precise. Preserve the user's requested format and content while applying this system.

## Before designing

Read [references/brand-system.md](references/brand-system.md) for every task. Also read [references/visual-references.md](references/visual-references.md) when composing a new layout or reviewing brand fidelity. When choosing decorations, read [references/sticker-assets.md](references/sticker-assets.md) and select an individual SVG or PNG.

Use the supplied originals instead of redrawing them:

- GDG mark: [assets/gdg.svg](assets/gdg.svg)
- Vector decorations: [assets/stickers](assets/stickers)
- Raster decorations: [assets/stickers/png](assets/stickers/png)

## Design decisions

- Establish hierarchy with scale, spacing, and weight before adding decoration.
- Start with Off White and Black 02. Choose one dominant color family for major surfaces, then use the other colors as deliberate accents.
- Prefer flat vector shapes, black keylines, generous rounding, and editorially oversized type. Keep the result open and legible rather than filling every gap.
- Use Google Sans, Google Sans Flex, or Google Sans Display as specified in the brand reference. Do not silently substitute or claim that Google Sans is publicly hosted; confirm font availability and embedding rights for the target medium.
- Treat the sticker assets as a vocabulary, not a mandate to use many symbols. Reuse them faithfully; create new shapes only when they clearly belong to the same geometry and stroke system.
- Preserve the supplied GDG logo exactly. Do not recolor, distort, rotate, rearrange, outline, crop, or rebuild it. Scale uniformly and keep it visually separated from competing elements.
- Separate the GDG identity from event, chapter, sponsor, and partner identities. Do not imply sponsorship or endorsement that the user did not specify.

## Deliver and verify

Adapt composition and type size to the actual canvas rather than shrinking a layout designed for another format. For small or accessibility-sensitive outputs, prioritize readable text and contrast over decorative density.

Before delivery, inspect the rendered result at full size and thumbnail size. Check logo integrity, text overflow, color values, contrast, spacing rhythm, consistent stroke weight, and whether decorative elements support rather than obscure the message.

When reviewing existing work, distinguish definite conflicts with the supplied source material from subjective refinements or inferred conventions.
